Joining Acme

We hire engineers who care about reliability and clear communication. Here is exactly what our interview process looks like.

How we interview

  1. Recruiter call (30 minutes): a conversation about your background and what you are looking for.
  2. Technical screen (60 minutes): live coding in TypeScript with one of our engineers, focused on practical problem solving.
  3. System design interview (60 minutes): design a payments or payouts service and discuss reliability and idempotency trade-offs.
  4. Values interview (45 minutes): behavioural questions about ownership, written communication and collaboration.
  5. Final conversation: meet the hiring manager and ask anything; offers follow within a week.

We do not use take-home assignments. We expect candidates to talk through trade-offs out loud during the interview stages.
